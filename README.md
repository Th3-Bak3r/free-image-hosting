## Image upload: get a shareable link

1. Open [HostMyImg](https://hostmyimg.com/) on your phone or computer.
2. Select your image or drop it into the uploader.
3. Wait for the upload to finish, then copy the direct image URL, BBCode, or HTML embed code.
4. Open the link to check the result and share it where you need it.

The uploader currently lists **PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, SVG, and BMP**, with a **10 MB** limit. Check the uploader for current restrictions.

## Temporary image hosting for short-term sharing

Use [temporary image hosting](https://hostmyimg.com/temporary-image-hosting) for a support screenshot, a draft design, or a reference you only need for a short project. Often called **temp image hosting**, this is useful when a picture has a limited sharing window.

Review the auto-delete options and retention conditions before uploading. Choose the setting that suits your task and keep the original file if you will need it again. An anonymous upload does not by itself guarantee a particular expiration time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is HostMyImg free to use?

HostMyImg offers free image uploads, subject to its upload limits and [terms of use](https://hostmyimg.com/terms-of-use).

### Do I need an account?

No account is required for anonymous uploads. Upload a supported file and copy the available sharing links.

### How do I turn a photo into a URL?

Open [photo to URL](https://hostmyimg.com/photo-to-url), upload your photo, and copy its direct link. Send that URL instead of attaching the file repeatedly.

### Are uploaded images private or stored forever?

Do not assume either. Uploading sends the file to the hosting service; anonymous uploading does not make it private. Review the [privacy policy](https://hostmyimg.com/privacy-policy) and retention conditions, and keep your own copies of important images.
